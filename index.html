How a fund gets its #1

  1. Pick a pool — on a category tab, funds only compete against others in that category (small caps vs small caps). The "All" view ranks everything in one list.
  2. Measure every parameter over the chosen window: expense ratio, Sharpe, info ratio (Alpha ÷ tracking error), volatility, CAGR, beta, Treynor, Jensen's alpha, selectivity and tracking error.
  3. Rank within the pool on each parameter. Being best on a parameter earns the most points on it (for cost and volatility "best" means lowest — that's the team's rule).
  4. Apply your weights (the sliders) and add them up. Highest total is rank 1 — the best fund is always at the top.
  5. Team default: five parameters are switched on, each at 20 — Sharpe, info ratio, volatility, 5-year CAGR and net selectivity (they add up to 100). The others start at 0 (off) so no one's ranking moves until they turn one on. Adjust sliders any time; "Share view" hands someone a link that opens with your weights.

Funds under a year old show a NEW badge: not enough history to trust a ranking.
